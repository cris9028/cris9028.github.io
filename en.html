Numerical solver using Brent's method for precise calculation of anchor points A, B1, B2 and lever arms R1 and R2 of the gas spring.

Automatic equilibrium check in both open and closed positions, with percentage error and convergence indicator for each configuration.

Catalog Search

Force, moment and operator effort charts across the full gas spring stroke. Realistic animated visualization of the mechanism in motion.
